The Joy of Convolution

A browser port of the Java applets by Steven Crutchfield, Johns Hopkins University (continuous time, Fall 1996; discrete time, Summer 1997, updated by Michael Ross, Fall 2001), from the JHU Signals, Systems, and Control Demonstrations directed by Wilson J. Rugh.

Work in progress: not for classroom use yet. Permission from the original authors is being sought.

  • The Joy of Convolution (continuous time)
  • The Joy of Convolution, discrete time (not ported yet)
